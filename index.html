Chapter 07
Detective Corner

Cases closed by data

💍

The September 2022 explosion — solved

The two biggest days in family history happened back-to-back: Sep 4 (322 messages) and Sep 5 (202). Top words: muhurtham, pics, looks, marriage, mahalaya, vakyam. A wedding match was being discussed and its date fixed from the panchang — with Amma firing 113 messages in a single day. One wedding, two all-time records.

🌕

The full-moon broadcast

The #2 day ever (Mar 20, 2022 — 250 messages) was an Arunachala full-moon visit: 193 of the 250 messages were photos and videos, led by Lakshmi and Ashwini Akka. Essentially a live outside-broadcast van, but it's your family.

🗣️

The retreating mother tongue

Telugu script by generation: Amma 8.7% of messages, Appa 7.4%, Mani Anna 5.7% — then a cliff — the younger generation, effectively 0%. A whole language shift, visible in one family's group chat.

📱

The vanishing

The unsaved +91 number peaked at 752 messages in 2023, then went completely silent from 2025. Changed numbers? Left the country? The data ends; the mystery doesn't.
